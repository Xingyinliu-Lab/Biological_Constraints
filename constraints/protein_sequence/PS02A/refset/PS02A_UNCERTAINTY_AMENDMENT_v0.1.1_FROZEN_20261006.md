# PS02A RefSet Contract — Uncertainty Amendment v0.1.1

**Status:** `FROZEN`  
**Date:** 2026-10-06

## Binding decision

The primary PS02A uncertainty resampling unit is the complete anchor-clan block. Each bootstrap draw samples anchor clans with replacement and carries every frozen bundle assigned to that clan. The frozen macro hierarchy is recomputed within each replicate.

\[
\boxed{\text{primary bootstrap unit} = \text{complete anchor-clan block}}
\]

Selected negative endpoints or parents reused across anchor clans are retained in a cross-clan reuse ledger. They are descriptive dependency diagnostics and prespecified sensitivity targets; they do not transitively merge anchor-clan bootstrap units.

## Rationale and scope

The former union-find closure combined anchor-clan blocks through shared negatives until all 158 clans formed one component. That result is retained as a withdrawn-rule diagnostic, not as the uncertainty procedure. This amendment is model-blind and leaves unchanged the 10,072 bundles, biological truth, identity/coverage matching, source membership and point estimator.

The Numeric Method must freeze draw count, seed, confidence-interval rule and the prespecified cross-clan-reuse sensitivity before formal model scoring. Historical anchor-clan intervals remain development-only and are not promoted by this amendment.
