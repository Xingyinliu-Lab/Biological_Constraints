# PS02A RefSet Contract — Matching-Policy Amendment v0.1.6

**Status:** `FROZEN`  
**Date:** 2026-10-06

## Binding decision

\[
\boxed{\text{primary hard matching} = \text{anchor-relative sequence identity} + \text{bilateral alignment coverage}.}
\]

Length, composition, taxonomy, annotation quality and sampling scope are source-side balance diagnostics and prespecified sensitivity/reporting axes. They do not determine membership in PS02A v1 primary. Any future promotion of a diagnostic axis to a hard gate requires a new preregistered RefSet-contract version and rematerialization before model evaluation.

## Authority effect

This amendment resolves the previous Word-to-machine mismatch: it aligns the Word-level primary policy with the existing machine implementation. It does not change biological truth, family/clan definitions, positive/negative roles, source universe, point estimator or the 16K selected membership.

A separate uncertainty amendment governs resampling.
